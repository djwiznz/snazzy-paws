Privacy policy

What we collect

  • Contact details you share (name, email, phone).
  • Booking details about your dog (name, breed, notes).

Why we collect it

We use this to confirm bookings, provide grooming services, and follow up if needed.

How long we keep it

We keep booking records only as long as needed for appointments and basic business records.

Sharing

We don’t sell personal data. We may use trusted providers to host this site and email; they process data only so we can run the service.

New Zealand privacy principles

We handle personal information in line with New Zealand’s Privacy Act 2020 and the 13 privacy principles set by the Office of the Privacy Commissioner. In short, we collect only what we need, tell you why, keep it safe, keep it only as long as we need it, let you see and correct it, and don’t share it without your okay or a lawful reason.

Read the principles here: privacy.org.nz/privacy-principles.

Where information is stored

We run a simple website. Any booking or contact details you send us are stored in our email or booking records. Some services we use may host data outside New Zealand (for example, our website host or Google Maps). We take reasonable steps to use reputable providers and settings that offer comparable safeguards.

Contact and complaints

If you have a privacy question, contact us at hello@snazzypaws.example. You can also contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner: privacy.org.nz.

Cookies and third-party content

Pages with an embedded Google Map may set cookies from Google. We don’t run ads or tracking pixels.

Your choices

You can ask us to update or remove your details. Contact: hello@snazzypaws.example
